06.01.26

Stop 1, Stop 2, Stop 3.

I just took two ibuprofen. My head is pounding.

Me and Sydney drove from Phoenix to Albuquerque, it probably took eight hours with some stops. We'll be here for the next two months or so, and we pick up Shane from the airport tonight. #Internship grind starts tomorrow.

Stop 1, approx. 13:30MST: In-N-Out

Yes this is the best fast food burger spot in America and I'm sorry if you've never experienced it (you're missing out). I ordered a 2x2, animal style, & fries. Water on the side.

Demolished my burger, probably took about four minutes. Keep in mind, my best time is two minutes and twenty-seven seconds... for a 4x4. For those that are unfamiliar, that is: two buns, four meat patties, four slices of cheese, caramelized grilled onions, pickles, and house spread.

I saw the liveliest tree ever outside of that In-N-Out.

Stop 2, approx. 14:22MST: Valley off the I-40

I spotted a lonesome tree. Had to visit.

It feels like every organism in this little valley is staring right at it. The shrubs, the rocks, the dead branches, even the sun chose to stand right above it. Everyone stares at him. He still stands tall nonetheless.

Stop 3, approx. 18:34MDT: Maverik gas station

Final stop of the trip. Found this very very odd structure.

What does it hide? Why does it lean ever so slightly to the right? Very beautiful red though. Casted some pretty neat shadows too.

Maverik gas stations remind me of my friends Max and Lucas. They visited me over Spring break and we had one right by the place we stayed. Our temporary home was a very quiet place. Very homey. Maverik was open late nights, so we stopped by a few times. We decided that Maverik knows us quite well.

My head is no longer pounding. Sydney and I picked up Shane. Now we are all eating Domino's pizza. Too much fast food today I think. I will start cooking very soon to avoid this from happening again.

The apartment we are staying in for the next two months is very spacious. It's beautifully furnished and I'm excited to learn to love this place. I'm getting that feeling like it's the first day of school tomorrow. Kind of nervous, excited to meet new people. I can tell though, that this time I am going to have here will be so fulfilling. Already feeling some sort of nostalgia somehow.

If you made it this far, thanks for reading. See u next time.

08.13.26

I'm on the plane back home now. I've already slept like five hours, and exactly like I predicted, time flew. Even this flight is flying.. hahaha.

Thank you Sydney for going on this trip with me, I think we will be friends forever. It has already been over a decade.

I think more than anything, I learn a lot about myself when I travel. Of course, I learn about the world and get to witness its wonders. I want to see every little nook and cranny that I possibly can. All of the unfathomably large canyons, and all of the teeny tiny critters. There's so little responsibility on trips, besides, doing it all. And when you're somewhere new, doing just that, then, your character is most on display. You probably won't run into anyone you know too well, and you don't have to worry about running into your old boss or your grandma's friend. So, how will you carry yourself? How do you want the people of a brand new, foreign place to perceive you? How do you want to be remembered in the relatively short time you're visiting. And I guess, who are you?

Some people say they know who they are, and some say they're clueless. No matter what side of this spectrum you're on, there will always be more. So I suggest you travel. Travel doesn't have to be far, it can be as nearby as a local restaurant you've brushed aside ever since you were young. Maybe travel can mean taking a new path home, or even, an old one. Experience all the new emotions and environments you can, and maybe, you'll get one step closer to an answer.
